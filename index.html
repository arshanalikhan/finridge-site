  • Sales Reporting: Maintain accurate and organized records of sales activities, customer interactions, and pipeline opportunities in the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system.

Qualifications

  • Proven track record in sales, preferably in credit card sales or financial services.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills to engage effectively with customers.
  • Persuasive negotiation and closing abilities to achieve sales targets.
  • Self-motivated and result-oriented with a passion for meeting and exceeding goals.
  • Ability to adapt to a dynamic and fast-paced sales environment.
  • Basic knowledge of financial products and services.

Accounts and Admin Manager

Job Description

We are looking for a highly organized and detail-oriented Accounts and Admin Manager to oversee the financial and administrative functions of our organization. As the Accounts and Admin Manager, you will be responsible for managing accounting processes, financial reporting, and administrative operations. Your role will play a critical part in ensuring the smooth and efficient functioning of the company’s financial and administrative systems.

Responsibilities

  • Manage Accounting Operations: Oversee day-to-day accounting activities, including accounts payable, accounts receivable, general ledger entries, and bank reconciliations.
  • Financial Reporting: Prepare accurate and timely financial reports, including profit and loss statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements. Present financial data to senior management for decision-making purposes.
  • Budgeting and Forecasting: Collaborate with department heads to develop and monitor budgets, track expenses, and provide financial forecasts.
  • Auditing and Compliance: Ensure adherence to financial regulations and company policies. Coordinate with auditors during internal and external audits.
  • Tax Compliance: Monitor tax compliance, prepare tax returns, and liaise with tax authorities when required.
  • Financial Analysis: Conduct financial analysis to identify trends, cost-saving opportunities, and areas for improvement.
  • Office Administration: Supervise administrative staff and oversee office operations, including maintenance, supplies, and equipment procurement.
  • HR Coordination: Assist with HR-related tasks, such as onboarding new employees, managing leave records, and coordinating employee benefits.
  • Vendor Management: Negotiate with vendors, review contracts, and ensure the timely processing of payments.
  • Records Management: Maintain and organize financial and administrative records, both physical and digital.
  • Compliance and Legal Requirements: Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements related to administration and record-keeping.
  • Process Improvement: Identify inefficiencies in administrative processes and implement improvements to enhance operational efficiency.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree in finance, accounting, business administration, or a related field.
  • Proven experience as an Accounts Manager, Finance Manager, or similar role.
  • Strong understanding of accounting principles, financial reporting, and budgeting.
  • Proficiency in accounting software and MS Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint).
  • Excellent organizational and time management skills.
  • Attention to detail and accuracy in financial reporting.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Effective communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Leadership skills to manage a team and collaborate with various departments.
  • Familiarity with HR and administrative processes is a plus.

Life at FinRidge

Work-life balance

Work-life balance is the delicate equilibrium between the demands of one’s professional life and personal well-being. In today’s fast-paced and competitive world, maintaining a healthy balance between work responsibilities and personal pursuits has become increasingly challenging. Achieving work-life balance is vital for overall well-being, mental health, and productivity, ensuring individuals can lead fulfilling and enriched lives.

Diversity and inclusion

Diversity and inclusion are essential principles that promote equality, respect, and acceptance of all individuals, regardless of their backgrounds, identities, or abilities. Embracing diversity means acknowledging and celebrating the unique perspectives and experiences that each person brings to the table. Inclusion, on the other hand, involves creating an environment where everyone feels valued, heard, and empowered to participate fully. Together, diversity and inclusion form the foundation of a thriving and harmonious workplace, community, and society at large.

Work hard, play hard

“Work hard, play hard” is a popular adage that encapsulates the idea of maintaining a healthy balance between diligent efforts in professional life and the pursuit of enjoyment and relaxation in personal life. It emphasizes the importance of dedication and productivity in work, coupled with the need to unwind and engage in leisure activities to recharge and find fulfillment.

Make an impact

Making an impact goes beyond personal achievements; it centers on leaving a positive mark on the lives of others and the environment. It involves empathy, compassion, and a sense of responsibility toward creating a world that is fair, just, and sustainable for future generations. Individuals who strive to make an impact recognize the interconnectedness of all beings and the shared responsibility to foster progress and well-being.
